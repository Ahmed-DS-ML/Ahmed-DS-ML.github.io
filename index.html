Who is Ahmed Ashraf?

Ahmed Ashraf is a data scientist and AI engineer who builds AI products that run real operations. Based in Cairo, Egypt, he works remotely with Deep Blue Startup Studios in Riyadh on AI automation and agentic systems. He focuses on applied, system-level solutions that integrate AI automation, agentic workflows, and product engineering for operators who need software that survives the shop floor. His products include TINDA, a Saudi automotive platform connecting AI diagnosis, spare parts, and checkout; QUBE, a digital commerce product from discovery to conversion; and Metron, an industrial AI operating system for live OEE and governed shop-floor action. He previously led analytics and patent-pending injection-machine automation at ElShorbagy for Plastics. He is an alumnus of Cairo University and Ain Shams University. He ships in Arabic and English, using n8n, RAG, Python, and ERP-linked factory intelligence. Still learning. Still building. Still sharing.
